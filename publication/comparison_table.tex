\section*{Short Interpretation}

The most directly comparable small-data/kernel papers are Doyle et al., Gopinath and Shanthi, Kadhim and Kamil, and He et al. They show that fixed texture descriptors and SVMs remain competitive when image counts are limited. Cai et al. is especially relevant because it uses the same broad texture families as our implementation, including GLCM, LBP, Gabor, and MRF, but it fuses them with deep AlexNet features. Our contribution is different: we build a broad analytic convolutional kernel bank, score and select kernels by discrimination and response diversity, and evaluate compact interpretable response sets against a CNN baseline.

\section*{Strict Small-Data Kernel Screening}
