# Master Thesis Writing Guide (Humanized, Anti-Plagiarism, Professional)

## 1. Writing Style (Humanized Academic Tone)

-   Use clear, natural academic language
-   Avoid robotic or repetitive phrasing
-   Vary sentence structure
-   Use discipline-specific terminology correctly
-   Maintain logical flow between paragraphs

## 2. Originality & Anti-Plagiarism

-   Always paraphrase instead of copying
-   Cite all external ideas, data, and quotes
-   Use multiple sources to synthesize insights
-   Avoid patchwriting (minor word changes)
-   Maintain your own analytical voice

## 3. Paraphrasing Techniques

-   Change sentence structure, not just words
-   Use synonyms carefully (preserve meaning)
-   Combine ideas from multiple sources
-   Add interpretation or critique

## 4. Professional Academic Structure

### Introduction

-   Research problem
-   Objectives
-   Research questions
-   Significance

### Literature Review

-   Thematic organization
-   Critical analysis (not summary)
-   Identify gaps

### Methodology

-   Research design
-   Data collection
-   Tools and methods
-   Justification of choices

### Results

-   Present findings clearly
-   Use tables/figures where needed

### Discussion

-   Interpret results
-   Compare with literature
-   Explain implications

### Conclusion

-   Summarize findings
-   Contributions
-   Limitations
-   Future research

## 5. Academic Integrity Practices

-   Use citation managers (Zotero, Mendeley)
-   Follow consistent citation style (GOST, APA, etc.)
-   Keep track of all sources أثناء writing

## 6. Language Quality

-   Avoid informal words (e.g., "a lot", "things")
-   Prefer precise vocabulary
-   Use active/passive voice appropriately
-   Proofread for grammar and clarity

## 7. Coherence & Flow

-   Use transition words:
    -   Therefore, However, Moreover, Consequently
-   Each paragraph = one main idea
-   Link paragraphs logically

## 8. Critical Thinking

-   Do not just describe → analyze
-   Question assumptions
-   Compare viewpoints
-   Justify arguments with evidence

## 9. Common Mistakes to Avoid

-   Plagiarism (direct or indirect)
-   Overuse of quotes
-   Lack of citations
-   Poor structure
-   Inconsistent terminology

## 10. Final Quality Check

-   Run plagiarism detection tools
-   Check grammar (Grammarly, etc.)
-   Ensure formatting consistency (GOST)
-   Verify all references are cited
